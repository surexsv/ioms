Excel / CSV import

Upload a file of invoices to validate, then confirm. Confirmed invoices are created as Approved (PDF available) without an order.

  • Formats: .xlsx or .csv
  • Maximum 500 rows and 5 MB
  • Customer Name must already exist in IOMS (exact name, case-insensitive). Customers are never auto-created.
  • GST % must be {{ gst_rate_percent }}% (company rate)
  • Enter common invoice details once. Extra particulars on the next rows can leave Invoice Number and Customer Name blank — they attach to the invoice above
  • Repeating the same Invoice Number also groups those rows into one invoice
  • A new invoice with a blank Invoice Number and a filled Customer Name is grouped by customer + PO + dates; a number is generated on confirm
  • Confirm is all-or-nothing — a failure rolls back every invoice in the batch
